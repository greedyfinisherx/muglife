THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE CUP

Hello. We're Mug Life.

Five college students, one shared idea, and plenty of reasons to take a coffee break.

OUR STORY

A small idea with a lot of heart.

We're a team of five college students from Quezon City who came together with a simple idea: a coffee shop where people can enjoy good coffee, good conversations, and a little happiness in their day.

As students, we know how busy life can get. Between classes, deadlines, and study sessions, sometimes a short break is all we need. That feeling inspired us to create Mug Life.

Whether you're catching up with friends or taking a moment for yourself, we hope our coffee shop can be a welcoming part of your day.
