About Us

Hello and Welcome to Mug Life!

We're a team of five college students from Quezon City who came together with one simple idea: to create a coffee shop where all people can enjoy good coffee, good conversations, and a little happiness in their everyday lives.

As students, we know how busy and stressful life can get. Between classes, deadlines, study sessions, and everything in between, sometimes what we need is a good cup of coffee and a place where we can slow down and relax for a while. That's what inspired us to create Mug Life Coffee Shop.

Mug Life is a small coffee shop created with passion and creativity. Whether you're grabbing coffee before class, studying with friends, taking a break from a busy day, or simply looking for a cozy place to relax, we hope Mug Life can be a place you can visit to make your day a little bit better.

Behind Mug Life are five students, one team, and a shared dream of creating something meaningful. Every cup we serve is made with our customers in mind, and every bit of your support means so much to us as students who are willing to learn, grow, and chase a dream together.
